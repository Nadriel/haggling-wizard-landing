How it works

  1. Add books to your wishlist

    Search for the out-of-print RPG and D&D titles you're hunting for and save them.

  2. We scan second-hand marketplaces daily

    Haggling Wizard checks eBay, AbeBooks, and Biblio every day for new listings.

  3. You get an email when a deal appears

    When a listing falls within your price range, we send you a direct link to the listing.
