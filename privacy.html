What we collect

The only personal information we collect is your email address, and only if you voluntarily submit it via the signup form on our home page. We use that address solely to notify you when Haggling Wizard launches. We do not sell, share, or rent your email address to anyone.

This site does not use cookies, tracking pixels, or analytics. We do not monitor your browsing behaviour. No third-party advertising networks have access to this site.

Haggling Wizard links to eBay, AbeBooks, and Biblio using affiliate links. When you click one of these links and make a purchase, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. Affiliate links do not affect our recommendations — we only surface listings relevant to your search.

Data retention

If you sign up for launch notifications, your email address is stored in Mailchimp until you unsubscribe. Every notification email will include an unsubscribe link. We will not contact you for any purpose other than the launch notification you signed up for.
